## Senior Software Engineer & Technical Lead

Hello! I'm **Livingstone Cano**, a Software Architect with over 15 years of experience transforming business needs into robust, scalable, and high-availability systems.

My focus is on leading technical teams and designing solutions under **Clean Architecture** principles, leveraging the **.NET 9/10** ecosystem and **Cloud** technologies.

### 🎯 My Value Proposition

My career is not just about writing code; it's about designing comprehensive technological strategies. I specialize in the financial and insurance sectors, where precision, security, and performance are non-negotiable.

I have had the opportunity to **lead and coordinate critical implementations** across **Honduras, Panama, Nicaragua, and Spain**, managing the adaptation of financial platforms to local regulations and high-standard international requirements.
